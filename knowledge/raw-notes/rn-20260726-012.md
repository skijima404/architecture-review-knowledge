# Capability Gaps Are Addressed Through Transition and Knowledge Transfer

## Note

This note records an interview response to a question in `rn-20260726-003`.

The capabilities required by a Target Architecture are determined from the
Architecture Vision rather than by simply limiting the target to current
organizational capability.

When capability is missing, this practitioner gives priority to a long-term
view, Transition Architecture, and Knowledge Transfer. The intent is to make
capability acquisition part of the change rather than treating the present
state as permanent.

Potential improvements may still be considered when they are not explicitly
named in the Architecture Vision. For example, a reasonable opportunity to
reduce operating cost may be worth adopting even when operating-cost
optimization is not a stated objective.

Such improvements are not automatically mandatory. Their priority depends on
the Architecture Vision, required delivery date, available cost, current
capability, and expected long-term benefit. An improvement that is excessive
for those constraints may remain staged or optional.
