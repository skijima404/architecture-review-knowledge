# Requirement Plausibility Is Checked Against Responsibility and Governance

## Note

This note records an interview response to a question in `rn-20260726-028`.

The practitioner may first notice an intuitive mismatch between a requirement
and the responsibility or governance needs of the group that supposedly
originated it.

The question is not only whether the requested feature is understandable. The
practitioner asks whether this type of requirement would naturally arise from
that group's business responsibility and whether the group has authority to
define the implied business policy.

In a generalized example, mobile workers experience a legitimate burden when
they cannot conveniently use a digital time-recording system. They might request
that attendance be recorded automatically using default working hours.

The operational pain belongs to the workers' context. Automatically declaring
working hours, however, changes an attendance and employment policy that may be
owned by a Human Resources or workforce-governance responsibility.

If the requesting group bypasses the policy owner and presses the system team to
implement the requested solution, the resulting Product Backlog Item can appear
inconsistent with the organization's responsibility path.

The PBI is therefore decomposed into:

- the observed user or operational pain;
- the requested system behavior;
- the business rule or policy implied by that behavior;
- the Domain that owns the policy;
- the party authorized to change it; and
- the path by which the request reached the Development Team.

Practitioner intuition triggers the investigation but does not by itself prove
that the requirement is invalid.

## Questions

- How should a Product Owner respond when a valid user pain implies a policy
  change owned by another Domain?
- What evidence confirms who owns the relevant governance decision?
- Which PBI fields would make requirement origin and decision authority visible?

## Next Steps

Capture the investigation and correction path after a responsibility mismatch
is detected.
