# DDD Boundary Validation Is Baseline; BAU Trace-Back Is Advanced Practice

## Note

This note refines the applicability boundary in `rn-20260726-030`.

Some practices identified in the interview should not be treated as specific to
this practitioner.

Architects designing Microservices should perform meaningful DDD and investigate
insufficient domain decomposition. In particular, an East-West interaction that
cannot be made asynchronous is a reason to question whether Business
Architecture, Bounded Contexts, responsibilities, or business states have been
decomposed sufficiently.

The practitioner-specific part is the depth and frequency of the trace-back.
When a technical or delivery symptom is observed, this practitioner readily
traces it through Business Architecture to the desired business-as-usual (BAU)
process, organizational responsibility, governance, and operating behavior.
They move repeatedly between Business Architecture and Technology Architecture
without treating that traversal as unusually expensive.

Many Technology-oriented architects may be able to perform this analysis but
cannot do it as lightly or as frequently. They may need collaboration with
Domain Experts, Product Management, Business Architecture, or other structured
support.

The distinction is therefore:

```text
Expected baseline
  -> perform DDD
  -> test domain and responsibility decomposition
  -> investigate suspicious East-West coupling

Advanced practitioner behavior
  -> trace observed symptoms back to desired BAU
  -> reexamine organizational responsibility and governance
  -> move repeatedly between Business and Technology Architecture
```

Reducing the frequency or depth of BAU trace-back may be appropriate for a
general method, but the conditions for doing so have not yet been defined.

## Questions

- Which signals must trigger deeper Business Architecture trace-back?
- Which checks can a Technology-oriented architect perform without reconstructing
  the complete BAU design?
- When should the architect involve a Domain Expert, Product Manager, or
  Business Architect?
- How should a lower-frequency method preserve decision quality?

## Next Steps

Develop a Candidate tiered review method from these questions. Do not present
such a method as the practitioner's established process until it has been
reviewed and tested.
