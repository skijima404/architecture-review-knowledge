# Architecture Assessment Precedes the DDD Workshop

## Note

A DDD workshop is not necessarily the beginning of an architecture
transformation.

When the practitioner owns the design process, an assessment is normally used
to observe the current business, systems, integrations, operations, and
organizational ownership before the workshop.

The assessment produces baseline observations and preliminary hypotheses. The
workshop then provides an opportunity to test and revise those hypotheses with
business knowledge. It does not need to create all boundary hypotheses from
zero.

Assessment evidence may include business direction, value streams, code-change
history, integration behavior, incidents, operational work, and the distribution
of knowledge and ownership.
