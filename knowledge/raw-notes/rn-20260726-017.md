# Cross-Context Knowledge Overlap Requires Investigation

## Note

This note records an unresolved interview response to a question in
`rn-20260726-004`.

When Domain Expert handover boundaries and Developer Team knowledge boundaries
do not align inside one Bounded Context, no general rule is currently asserted.
The appropriate response is considered case-dependent and requires a concrete
example.

When substantial Domain Knowledge appears to overlap across different Bounded
Contexts, the practitioner investigates at least two hypotheses:

- an intentional shared model may exist and could require a Shared Kernel; or
- responsibilities or Domain Knowledge may be unintentionally mixed across
  invalid or eroded boundaries.

The investigation must occur before deciding which organizational or
development boundary should take precedence.

## Questions

- What evidence distinguishes an intentional Shared Kernel from accidental
  boundary mixing?
- How should team boundaries be evaluated when handover and knowledge boundaries
  differ inside one Bounded Context?

## Next Steps

Keep both questions open until a concrete case can be examined.
