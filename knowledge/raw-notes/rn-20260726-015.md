# Team Type Determines the Required Domain Knowledge Scope

## Note

This note records an interview response to a question in `rn-20260726-004`.

This reasoning is applied during Microservices decomposition and team design. It
is based on practitioner experience and simulation rather than observation of a
team already becoming overloaded.

The appropriate Domain Knowledge scope depends on the team type.

A Complicated Subsystem Team needs deep knowledge of the subsystem's inherent
complexity. Unrelated business context should be minimized so that the team can
focus on that complexity. Full Value Stream detail may be useful but is not
necessarily required.

A Stream-aligned Team needs the business context of its complete Value Stream.
It needs minimal detail about unrelated Value Streams. When it depends on a
Complicated Subsystem, it should receive the input, output, contract, and
semantics needed for its responsibility rather than the subsystem's internal
detail.

Knowledge load is therefore evaluated by asking whether information is directly
required for implementation and ownership, and whether it is provided at the
appropriate abstraction level for that team type. Developer utilization is not
the primary optimization target.
