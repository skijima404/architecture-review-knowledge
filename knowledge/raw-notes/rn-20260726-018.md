# Historical Evidence and Intent Reconstruction Explain Code Complexity

## Note

This note records an interview response to a question in `rn-20260726-005`.

The practitioner compares present code complexity with Git history and other
change records. This helps distinguish complexity present in the original
design from complexity accumulated through later modifications.

The practitioner also begins with a charitable reconstruction:

> If this design decision contained some validity, under what conditions would
> it have been reasonable?

The resulting hypothesis is checked through interviews with people who
understand the decision or its historical context.

The depth of investigation is difficult to define mechanically. This
practitioner tends to stop when they can empathize with and coherently explain
the situation that produced the design. That stopping heuristic depends on
personal experience and confidence in empathetic interpretation; it must not be
treated as independent validation.

## Questions

- What evidence should complement empathetic reconstruction?
- How can another reviewer challenge or reproduce the reconstructed design
  intent?
- What additional evidence indicates responsibility or boundary misalignment
  rather than Engineering Debt?

## Next Steps

Continue the interview on evidence for boundary misalignment.
