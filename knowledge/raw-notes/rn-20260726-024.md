# East-West Synchrony Triggers Business-Architecture Reexamination

## Note

This note corrects and extends `rn-20260726-023`. Public source
`src-20260726-001` provides an authored explanation of the reasoning.

The practitioner begins from the common Microservices principle that
North-South client interactions commonly use request-response APIs, while
East-West service integration should prefer asynchronous communication to
protect service autonomy.

The principle is used as a falsification question:

> This is East-West interaction. Why can it not be asynchronous?

An inability to use asynchronous interaction may reflect a genuine business
constraint. It may also indicate that Business Architecture has not been
decomposed sufficiently.

Technical objections such as required ordering, real-time processing,
compensation difficulty, and consistency risk are translated into business
questions:

- Which state must the user see immediately?
- Which downstream work may complete later?
- Where is processing order a real business invariant?
- What inconsistency or delay is tolerable?
- Which business outcome must remain atomic from the user's perspective?

Answering those questions can reveal independently meaningful business states
and allow downstream responsibilities to react autonomously. The reasoning
moves from Technology Architecture back to Business Architecture, then through
Application and Data boundaries, and finally returns to Technology Architecture
to test whether asynchronous implementation is feasible.

The practitioner does not force asynchronous communication after a genuine
synchronous business invariant has been established. The default creates a
reason to investigate and improve the decomposition.

## Questions

- What evidence confirms a genuinely synchronous business invariant?
- How should user-visible states be modeled so that asynchronous downstream work
  remains understandable?
- When does asynchronous implementation complexity outweigh the autonomy it
  provides?

## Next Steps

Capture a generalized example that begins with a synchronous assumption and
discovers separable business states.
