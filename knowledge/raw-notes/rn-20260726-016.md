# Cross-Team Domain Knowledge Is Shared at Relationship-Specific Granularity

## Note

This note records an interview response to a question in `rn-20260726-004`.

All development teams need a high-level picture of the system and its business
context. Detailed Domain Knowledge should then be distributed according to each
team's responsibility and relationships.

When one team provides an API and another consumes it, knowledge exchange is
bidirectional. The consumer needs the provider Domain's high-level purpose, the
API's business semantics, and its behavior. The provider needs enough of the
consumer's business intent to understand the desired outcome and usage
conditions.

Team interaction therefore makes some knowledge exchange unavoidable. The goal
is not to eliminate shared knowledge, but to provide the knowledge needed for
the relationship without transferring unnecessary internal detail.

The practitioner currently hypothesizes that multiple teams requiring the same
high density of Domain Knowledge may occur when they operate in the same Value
Stream and modify the same Microservice. This is not yet a general rule.
